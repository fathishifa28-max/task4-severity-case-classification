# ADR Case Analysis

## Case 1 – Amoxicillin

- **Drug:** Amoxicillin
- **Reaction:** Skin rash and itching
- **Seriousness:** Non-Serious
- **Expectedness:** Expected
- **Clinical Impact:** Mild
- **Reason:** The reaction is mild and does not meet serious outcome criteria.

## Case 2 – Ibuprofen

- **Drug:** Ibuprofen
- **Reaction:** Gastrointestinal bleeding requiring hospitalization
- **Seriousness:** Serious
- **Expectedness:** Expected
- **Clinical Impact:** Severe
- **Reason:** Hospitalization makes the event serious.

## Case 3 – Metformin

- **Drug:** Metformin
- **Reaction:** Lactic acidosis
- **Seriousness:** Serious
- **Expectedness:** Unexpected
- **Clinical Impact:** Severe
- **Reason:** Lactic acidosis is potentially life-threatening and requires urgent medical intervention.

## Case 4 – Paracetamol

- **Drug:** Paracetamol
- **Reaction:** Mild nausea
- **Seriousness:** Non-Serious
- **Expectedness:** Expected
- **Clinical Impact:** Mild
- **Reason:** Mild nausea without serious medical consequences is classified as non-serious.

## Case 5 – Penicillin

- **Drug:** Penicillin
- **Reaction:** Anaphylactic reaction requiring emergency treatment
- **Seriousness:** Serious
- **Expectedness:** Expected
- **Clinical Impact:** Severe
- **Reason:** Anaphylaxis is potentially life-threatening and requires immediate medical intervention.

---

## Overall Assessment

Three cases are classified as serious and two cases as non-serious.

The serious cases require greater clinical attention because they involve hospitalization, potentially life-threatening conditions, or emergency medical intervention.
