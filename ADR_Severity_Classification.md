# Task 4 – Severity & Case Classification

## Objective

The objective of this task is to classify adverse drug reactions (ADRs) according to seriousness and expectedness and to evaluate their potential clinical impact using pharmacovigilance principles.

## Definitions

### Serious Adverse Drug Reaction

An adverse drug reaction is considered serious when it results in one or more important outcomes such as:

- Death
- Life-threatening condition
- Hospitalization or prolongation of hospitalization
- Persistent or significant disability/incapacity
- Congenital anomaly/birth defect
- Other medically important condition requiring medical intervention

### Non-Serious Adverse Drug Reaction

A non-serious ADR does not meet the regulatory criteria for a serious adverse event. It may cause discomfort or require treatment but does not result in the serious outcomes listed above.

### Expected ADR

An ADR is considered expected when its nature, severity, or outcome is consistent with the applicable reference safety information, such as the approved product information.

### Unexpected ADR

An ADR is unexpected when its nature, severity, or specificity is not consistent with the applicable reference safety information.

# Case 1 – Amoxicillin

**Adverse Reaction:** Skin rash and itching

**Seriousness:** Non-Serious

**Expectedness:** Expected

**Clinical Impact:** Mild

### Rationale

Skin rash and itching are recognized reactions associated with amoxicillin. When the reaction does not result in hospitalization, disability, life-threatening illness, or another serious outcome, it is classified as non-serious.

---

# Case 2 – Ibuprofen

**Adverse Reaction:** Gastrointestinal bleeding requiring hospitalization

**Seriousness:** Serious

**Expectedness:** Expected

**Clinical Impact:** Severe

### Rationale

Gastrointestinal bleeding can be associated with NSAID therapy. Because the patient requires hospitalization, the case meets a seriousness criterion.

---

# Case 3 – Metformin

**Adverse Reaction:** Lactic acidosis requiring urgent medical treatment

**Seriousness:** Serious

**Expectedness:** Unexpected

**Clinical Impact:** Severe

### Rationale

Lactic acidosis is a potentially life-threatening condition requiring urgent medical intervention. Therefore, the case is classified as serious. Expectedness should be determined by comparison with the applicable reference safety information.

---

# Case 4 – Paracetamol

**Adverse Reaction:** Mild nausea

**Seriousness:** Non-Serious

**Expectedness:** Expected

**Clinical Impact:** Mild

### Rationale

Mild nausea without hospitalization, disability, life-threatening consequences, or other serious outcomes is classified as non-serious. Nausea is a recognized adverse effect of medicines and should be considered expected when listed in the applicable reference safety information.

---

# Case 5 – Penicillin

**Adverse Reaction:** Anaphylactic reaction requiring emergency treatment

**Seriousness:** Serious

**Expectedness:** Expected

**Clinical Impact:** Severe

### Rationale

Anaphylaxis is a potentially life-threatening reaction that requires immediate medical intervention. Therefore, the case meets the seriousness criterion.

## Conclusion

The cases demonstrate how ADR reports can be evaluated using seriousness, expectedness, and clinical impact. Proper classification supports consistent pharmacovigilance assessment and prioritization of cases requiring further evaluation.
